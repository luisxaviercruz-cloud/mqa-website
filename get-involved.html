Why Get Involved?

When you support MQA+, you help:

  • Create safe and affirming spaces
  • Provide housing, resources, and support services
  • Empower LGBTQ+ youth and young adults
  • Strengthen Latinx, immigrant, BIPOC, and Hispanic communities
  • Build a more inclusive and equitable Northeast Ohio
