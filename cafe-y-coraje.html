Café y Coraje

Real conversations. Real support. Real community.

Café y Coraje is a welcoming space for LGBTQ+ individuals, especially our Latinx/Hispanic, immigrant, and BIPOC community, to connect, share, and uplift one another through conversation, resources, and community care.

Welcome to Café y Coraje

Café y Coraje brings LGBTQ+ individuals together, especially members of our Latinx/Hispanic, immigrant, and BIPOC communities, to build connections and support one another.

Our gatherings create room for real conversation, shared experiences, friendship, and community care. We meet through activities at different locations, giving members opportunities to connect in new ways and enjoy time together.

Whether you are joining us for the first time or reconnecting with familiar faces, you are welcome here. Come as you are, share what feels comfortable, and be part of a community where everyone belongs.

Our activities may look different from month to month, but the purpose stays the same: conversation, support, and meaningful community connections.

Our Community, Our Impact

Through Café y Coraje, we are creating welcoming spaces to build friendships, share resources, and strengthen connections across our community.
