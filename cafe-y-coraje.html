Café y Coraje

Real conversations. Real support. Real community.

Café y Coraje is a welcoming space for LGBTQ+ individuals, especially our Latinx/Hispanic, immigrant, and BIPOC community, to connect, share, and uplift one another through conversation, resources, and community care.
