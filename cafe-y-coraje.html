COMMUNITY GROUP

Café y Coraje

Real conversations. Real support. Real community.

Café y Coraje is a welcoming space for LGBTQ+ individuals, especially our Latinx/Hispanic, immigrant, and BIPOC community, to connect, share, and uplift one another through conversation, resources, and community care.

Café y Coraje is a monthly community group created by MQA+ to bring together LGBTQ+ Latinx and Hispanic individuals, including those of mixed racial and cultural backgrounds, to connect, share experiences, and support one another through conversation, community activities, and cultural celebration.

Each gathering offers a welcoming space to share stories, celebrate our cultures, build friendships, and find support. Allies are welcome to join, connect, and enjoy the community.

There is power in showing up, sharing our stories, and lifting each other up.

Join Our Community Group Meetups

Café y Coraje and QTBIPOC Kickbacks come together twice a month for shared activities, giving members of both groups a chance to meet new people, build friendships, and enjoy community together. Everyone is welcome, including allies.
