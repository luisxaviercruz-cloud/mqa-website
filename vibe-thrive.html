About Vibe & Thrive

Vibe & Thrive is MQA+’s annual community health initiative created to promote wellness, prevention, and connection within our LGBTQ+ community, with a focus on Latinx/Hispanic, BIPOC, and immigrant communities. The event brings together health resources, wellness activities, movement, and community support in a safe, inclusive, and welcoming environment.
