ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Health, Wellness & Community—Together.

Vibe & Thrive Community Health is MQA+’s annual May initiative focused on making health and wellness resources feel welcoming, accessible, and connected to community.

Each gathering brings together community organizations, wellness activities, movement, health resources and opportunities for connection in one inclusive environment. The goal is not only to share information, but to create a positive experience where people can engage with wellness in ways that feel supportive and approachable.

Health Resources

Connect with community health information, prevention resources, screenings and participating organizations.

Wellness Activities

Explore activities that support physical, emotional and overall well-being.

Movement & Fitness

Make movement approachable through group activities, fitness and opportunities to get active together.

Community Connection

Build relationships, discover local resources and enjoy a welcoming space centered on belonging.

WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

A Community Health Experience

Vibe & Thrive combines practical resources with activities and community connection so wellness can be experienced—not just talked about.
