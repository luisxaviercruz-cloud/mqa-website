COMMUNITY GROUP

QTBIPOC Kickbacks

Community. Culture. Good Vibes.

QTBIPOC Kickbacks is an MQA+ community group bringing together queer and trans Black, Indigenous, and People of Color to connect, share experiences, build friendships, and enjoy community activities together.

Each gathering offers a welcoming space to relax, celebrate our identities, share resources, and build community. Allies are welcome to join, connect, and enjoy the community.

Real people. Real conversations. Real community.
