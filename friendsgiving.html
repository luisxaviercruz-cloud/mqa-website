About the Initiative

Friendsgiving Community Dinner was created to provide a welcoming place where people can come together, share a meal, and experience a sense of community and belonging.

Launched in 2025, Friendsgiving especially supports individuals who may be without nearby family or a strong support system, as well as those facing barriers to enjoying a holiday meal. What began as an annual community dinner is growing into a broader initiative centered on food, connection, resources, and community support.

Beginning in 2027, Friendsgiving will expand through quarterly gatherings, allowing us to carry that spirit of connection and support beyond the holiday season.

More Than a Meal

Friendsgiving is about creating a place where people feel welcomed, supported, and connected. While sharing a meal brings us together, the heart of this initiative is making sure no one has to experience the season—or difficult moments throughout the year—without a sense of community.

Our gatherings create opportunities to meet new people, build meaningful connections, and access additional support when available. As Friendsgiving grows, we hope to offer more than a shared meal by incorporating groceries, clothing, community resources, and other forms of support based on the needs of each gathering.

Second Annual Friendsgiving Dinner

November 2026

Our second annual dinner will follow the same format as last year—an inclusive community meal for those who may not have nearby family, a support system, or the means to afford a holiday meal.

Quarterly Community Gatherings

Starting in 2027

Beginning in 2027, Friendsgiving will expand to quarterly gatherings throughout the year. Along with a shared meal, gatherings may include groceries or pantry items, donated clothing, and connections to community resources when available.
