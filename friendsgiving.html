About the Initiative

Friendsgiving Community Dinner is an inclusive initiative created to bring together LGBTQ+ individuals and community members who may not have nearby family, a support system, or the means to enjoy a holiday meal. What started in 2025 as an annual dinner is growing into a year-round initiative that creates space for connection, support and belonging.

More Than a Meal

Friendsgiving is about more than food. It is a welcoming space to share a meal, build connections and celebrate community—especially for those without nearby family or support.

A Welcoming MealCommunity ConnectionSupport & Resources

Second Annual Friendsgiving Dinner

November 2026

Our second annual dinner will follow the same format as last year—an inclusive community meal for those who may not have nearby family, a support system, or the means to afford a holiday meal.

Quarterly Community Gatherings

Starting in 2027

Beginning in 2027, Friendsgiving will expand to quarterly gatherings throughout the year. Along with a shared meal, gatherings may include groceries or pantry items, donated clothing, and connections to community resources when available.
