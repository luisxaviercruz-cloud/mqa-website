Who We Are

The Multi Cultural Queer+ Association (MQA+) is a community-centered nonprofit organization that creates safe spaces, resources, programs and opportunities for LGBTQ+ people — with a focus on Latinx, immigrant, BIPOC and other underserved communities.

Learn More About MQA+ →

Our Mission

To create culturally affirming spaces, resources and opportunities that support the well-being, growth and empowerment of LGBTQ+ people, with a focus on Latinx, immigrant, BIPOC and underserved communities.

Our Vision

A more inclusive, equitable and compassionate community where every LGBTQ+ person can thrive, feel a true sense of belonging and have access to the support they deserve.

Our Purpose

To uplift our community through advocacy, education, culturally relevant programs and collaborative partnerships that remove barriers and create opportunity.

Our Story

Rooted in Culture. Driven by Community.

MQA+ was founded in 2024 by Luis Cruz and T’Relle Smith with a shared vision: to create a safe, supportive and empowering space for LGBTQ+ individuals who often feel unseen, unheard or left behind.

Our lived experiences, cultural roots and commitment to community drive our work.
