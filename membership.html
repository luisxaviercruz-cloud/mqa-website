Active members receive access to two Community Group Meetup activities each month as part of their membership.

Active members receive an automatic $5 member benefit on eligible MQA+ ticketed events.

Digital Membership Card

When membership is activated, a digital membership card will be issued automatically and designed to work with MQA+'s Zeffy membership and check-in process.

Your account stays with you. If a paid membership ends or becomes inactive, the free My MQA+ account and appropriate history remain available. Paid member benefits pause until membership is active again.

Everything in One Place

My MQA+ is being designed as the central community and organization portal, with information and tools shown according to each person's role and permissions.

Membership

Membership status, recurring dues, benefits, payment history and digital membership card.

Events & Community Groups

Browse and register for events, view registrations and track included monthly Community Group activities.

Volunteer Center

Find opportunities, sign up for service and keep a record of volunteer participation and hours.

Jobs & Careers

Explore MQA+ and approved partner job opportunities, manage applications and track application status.

Resources & Support

Access community resources and, as the secure system grows, private assistance requests and appropriate support services.

Documents & Policies

Role-based access to resources such as the Employee Handbook, bylaws, governance documents, forms and acknowledgments.

Employee & Leadership Center

Authorized employees, board members and leadership can access the information and documents appropriate to their roles.

Training & Forms

Required training, certifications, waivers, agreements, acknowledgments and electronic forms in one location.

My Profile & Calendar

Manage account information, preferences, registrations, volunteer shifts and other upcoming MQA+ activity.
