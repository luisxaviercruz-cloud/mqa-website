Latin Pride Project

Culture. Community. Pride. Poder Juntos.

Latin Pride Project is our initiative to celebrate, uplift, and empower Latinx LGBTQ+ individuals through culture, connection, and collective action. We honor our heritage, embrace our full identities, and create spaces where our community can thrive.

WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

Marisol Martinez

One float. A historic moment. A lasting vision.

In 2022, Marisol Martinez spearheaded the first Latino Pride float in Cleveland’s Puerto Rican Parade. She wanted to bring greater awareness and visibility to LGBTQ+ people within the Latino community, creating a moment where culture and identity could be celebrated together.

Her work opened space for connection and pride—and laid the foundation for what would become the Latin Pride Project.

OUR STORY

From a parade float to a community initiative

2022

A historic first

Marisol Martinez brings the first Latino Pride float to Cleveland’s Puerto Rican Parade, making space for LGBTQ+ visibility and cultural pride.

2024

Carrying the vision forward

MQA+ takes over the Latin Pride Project, building on the foundation Marisol created and developing it into an ongoing community initiative.

TODAY

More than one day

The project continues to celebrate Latinx LGBTQ+ identities through culture, community connection, and opportunities to participate throughout the year.

CARRYING THE VISION FORWARD

Latin Pride Project honors Marisol’s original vision while creating more ways for Latinx LGBTQ+ people to feel seen, connected, and celebrated beyond the parade.
