Latin Pride Project

Nuestra Cultura. Nuestra Gente. Nuestro Poder.

The Latin Pride Project uplifts and empowers LGBTQ+ Latinx and Hispanic individuals through culturally relevant programs, resources and community spaces. We celebrate our identities, honor our roots and build a stronger, healthier future together.

BLK-QUEER COLLECTIVE

Black. Queer. Connected. Empowered.

The BLK-QUEER COLLECTIVE centers the experiences, voices and leadership of Black LGBTQ+ individuals. Through community, culture and collective action, we create space for joy, healing, opportunity and liberation.
