Latin Pride Project

Nuestra Cultura. Nuestra Gente. Nuestro Poder.

The Latin Pride Project uplifts and empowers LGBTQ+ Latinx and Hispanic individuals through culturally relevant programs, resources and community spaces. We celebrate our identities, honor our roots and build a stronger, healthier future together.

BLK-QUEER COLLECTIVE

Black. Queer. Connected. Empowered.

The BLK-QUEER COLLECTIVE centers the experiences, voices and leadership of Black LGBTQ+ individuals. Through community, culture and collective action, we create space for joy, healing, opportunity and liberation.

Friendsgiving Community Dinner

Good Food. Community. Belonging.

Launched in 2025, our annual Friendsgiving Community Dinner brings LGBTQ+ individuals and neighbors together for a welcoming holiday meal, connection and community—especially those without nearby family or support. Our second annual dinner takes place in November 2026, with plans to expand into quarterly community gatherings in 2027.

Vibe & Thrive Community Health

Your Wellness Matters.

Vibe & Thrive is MQA+’s annual community health initiative held every May, bringing together health and wellness resources, screenings, movement, fitness, mental wellness support and community connection in an inclusive and welcoming environment.
